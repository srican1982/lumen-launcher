Lumen privacy and data sharing

Updated October 9, 2026. Lumen stores launcher preferences, tasks, notes, favorites, app launch history, trips and sticker packs on your device. It has no advertising or analytics SDK.

Optional access

Contacts help you find people and make call reminders. Calendar access displays events and account/calendar names. Call history and notification access show missed calls, messages, badges and media controls. Usage access suggests apps from device usage history. Photo access and optional location access organize your trip album. Original photos are not deleted when you delete an album.

AI

AI uses the provider selected by your own key: Gemini or OpenRouter. AI voice sends your spoken request, relevant app and folder names, alarm times and recent voice conversation. Focus suggestions send the focus task text and candidate app names. Contact names are sent only if you separately enable that option. AI inbox summaries are off by default. If enabled, notification app names, titles and short previews are sent while Flow is visible. Hidden and private apps, secret notifications and numeric verification codes are excluded. These filters cannot guarantee removal of all sensitive information; leave summaries off for sensitive inboxes. Provider processing follows that provider's terms and privacy policy. API keys are encrypted using Android Keystore and excluded from backup.

Voice and photos

Hey Lumen is off by default and listens only while Home is visible. Wake recognition prefers an available on-device recognizer, with offline preference on other engines. Your device's speech provider may use network recognition. Lumen does not upload voice recordings itself. Photo background removal uses the on-device Google ML Kit model; the model may need a download. Save and share send only the image you choose. Sticker packs are exposed read-only to authorized WhatsApp callers.

Network services

Weather requests use Open-Meteo. With location permission, approximate coordinates are used. Without permission, ipwho.is estimates the location from your IP address. News requests use the selected publisher feeds. These services receive your IP address and the request needed to provide results.

Screen lock and alarms

The optional accessibility service is used only to lock the screen when requested; it does not read screen content or monitor actions. On older Android versions, Device Administrator is an optional fallback and requires a PIN/password after locking. Minimal alarm state is stored in device-protected storage so alarms work before the first unlock after a reboot. Ringing runs as a foreground service and stops after ten minutes.

Control and deletion

You can disable AI sharing, remove the saved key and revoke permissions in settings. Delete tasks, trip albums and sticker packs in Lumen. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes Lumen's local data; original photos and anything already shared remain in their destination apps. Android backup is restricted to the listed Focus preferences, not your API key or launcher data.
